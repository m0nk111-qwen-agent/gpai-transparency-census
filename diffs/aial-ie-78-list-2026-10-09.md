## 3b. Data-quality finding about the source (dated 2026-10-09)

**aial.ie's "archived version can be found here" links are broken for readers.** The list page
(`…/gpai-training-transparency/list_summaries`) emits RELATIVE links `../../archive/<Name>_<date>.pdf`,
which resolve to site-root `https://aial.ie/archive/<Name>_<date>.pdf` → **HTTP 404** (verified with
redirect-following, 2026-10-09). The actual files live at
`https://aial.ie/research/gpai-training-transparency/archive/<Name>_<date>.pdf` → HTTP 200 (verified
Adobe_Firefly_2026_07_22.pdf, 1.1 MB). Classic relative-path bug: anyone clicking their archive link
hits a 404 until they reconstruct the nested path. Implication for us: treat aial.ie archive URLs as
UNVERIFIED until normalized to the nested path; our census rows should store the working URL.
